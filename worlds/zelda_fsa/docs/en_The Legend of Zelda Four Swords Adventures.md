# Zelda Four Swords Adventures Multiworld Game Info

## Changes to the Game

- Intro cutscene is skipped
- You start with stage 1 of each level unlocked
- Unlock more stages with Ticket items 
	- To unlock 7-2 and 7-3 you need both the ticket, all 4 Royal Jewels and 6 maidens
	- To unlock 8-2 and 8-3, you need both the ticket, all 4 Royal Jewels and all 7 maidens)
- Every chest will visually contain 1 gem
- The game remembers what chests were opened and what item pedestals were emptied
- Keys and Moon Pearls are deleted (and NPC's giving those)
	- Received Keys/Moon Pearls will open their doors/Moon Gates
- You can carry all Key Items at once. Use D-pad L/R (disabled it's normal function)
- You will get notifications in-game for every/most items you receive

## Checks

- All Chests (excl. Big Chest) (401)
- All Item Pedestals (if enabled) (301)
- Royal Jewels & Dark Mirror (5)
- Beating a Stage (23)

## Items

### Progression
- Progressive Key Item (e.g. Bombs, 2 of each)
- Blue Bracelet
- Power Bracelet
- Heart Pieces (32 - Starting Max Health)
- Progressive Level x Ticket (2 per Level)
- 4 Royal Jewels
- 7 Maidens
- 1 of every Key, Moon Pearl, Book, or Letter (some act as progressive if they are required in order) (66 total)

### Traps

- Tingle Trap (Tingle will take away some of your Force Gems)
- Fire Trap (Link will be set on fire)

### Filler

Remaining slots are randomized 'junk' items. Pool consists of:
- Small, Normal & Big versions of the Green, Blue, Red & Purple Force Gems
- Force Fairy
- Heart
- Nothing

## Goal

Beat Palace of Winds (Defeat Gannon and get to the save popup)
This requires:
- 2 Progressive Level 8 Ticket
- The 4 Royal Jewels
- The 7 Maidens
- 4 Palace of Winds Progressive Moon Gate
- Palace of Winds Progressive 2F North Key
- Bow
- Either:
	- Roc's Feather
	- Pegasus Boots L2
- Either:
	- Bombs
	- Palace of Winds Progressive 2F North Key
